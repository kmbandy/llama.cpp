THE DRAFT CAPTURE IS CORRUPTED; THE PROBE WAS TRAINED ON A RANK-2
MATRIX. The §4 falsifier has NOT actually tested the learned
predictor. Chain of evidence, each step reproducible from the scripts
in falsifier-2026-08-09/:

1. Cross-block embeddings bit-identical: p95 of cross-block cosine =
   1.0000 exactly; 67% of 500 random blocks share identical leading
   512 floats. The buffer is STALE across capture calls.
2. Row 2 of every nd=2 block is fabricated: cos(row0,row1) = 0.5000
   exactly, norms 128.0 / 64.0 exactly — the arithmetic signature of
   "row1 = row0 truncated to 4096 dims + zeros". The writer over-reads
   a buffer that does not hold one 16384-float row per drafted token.
3. The "live" back half (dims 8192:16384) clusters by token identity
   (same-token cos 0.9997 vs 0.806 random) BUT:
4. SVD: rank 1 = 86.2% of variance, rank 2 = 100.00%. EFFECTIVE RANK
   2. Two scalars per sample. Nothing else in the whole 2.24 GB file.
